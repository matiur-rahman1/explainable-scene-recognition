\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

Please find enclosed our manuscript entitled \textbf{``Explainable Deep Learning for Scene Classification and Pattern Recognition: A Cross-Paradigm Benchmark and Attribution-Consistent Adversarial Defense''} by MD Matiur Rahman and Huanjie Tao, which we are submitting for consideration for publication as a Regular Paper in \emph{Neurocomputing} (Subscription Mode, \$0 APC).

Deep scene recognition models spanning classical Convolutional Neural Networks (ResNet-50, EfficientNet-B0), modernized ConvNets (ConvNeXt-Tiny), hierarchical Vision Transformers (Swin-Tiny), and contrastive Vision-Language Models (CLIP ViT-B/16) are predominantly evaluated on clean top-1 accuracy, post-hoc visual explainability, or adversarial robustness in isolation. Moreover, standard class activation maps suffer from spatial pooling dilution on shifted-window token grids, while standard adversarial training allows internal spatial attributions to drift under perturbation.

To address these gaps, our manuscript makes four contributions directly aligned with the scope of \emph{Neurocomputing}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Six-Architecture Cross-Paradigm Benchmark:} We evaluate ResNet-50, EfficientNet-B0, ConvNeXt-Tiny, Swin-Tiny, Zero-Shot CLIP ViT-B/16, and Linear-Probe CLIP ViT-B/16 across multiple random seeds on Intel Natural Scenes and a deterministic 100-class SUN397 protocol with paired McNemar statistical significance tests.
    \item \textbf{Variance-Gated LayerCAM (VG-LayerCAM):} We formulate a novel closed-form gradient attribution method combining second-order gradient-energy channel gating with element-wise spatial gradient weighting, evaluated via Insertion AUC, Deletion AUC, and the unified Faithfulness Gap ($\Delta\text{AUC}$).
    \item \textbf{Spatial Attribution-Consistent Adversarial Training (SAC-PGD-AT):} We propose a single-pass spatial energy consistency regularizer ($\beta \|\bar{E}(x) - \bar{E}(x_{\text{PGD}})\|_F^2$) that suppresses adversarial CAM attention drift while preserving clean and $\text{PGD-}L_\infty$ accuracy, supported by a systematic hyperparameter ablation study on $\beta$.
    \item \textbf{Single-Step Loss-Surface Overshooting Diagnosis & Open-Source Reproducibility:} We diagnose why single-step FGSM and $\text{FGSM}_{\text{pred}}$ overshoot curved label-smoothed loss surfaces compared to strictly monotonic 10-step $\text{PGD-}L_\infty$, and release our complete PyTorch framework and interactive Gradio diagnostic tool.
\end{enumerate}

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere, is not under consideration by any other journal, and has been approved by all authors.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration.
